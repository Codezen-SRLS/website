# C2X security audit

C2X is a blockchain gaming platform; this audit covered its CosmWasm contracts on Terra: a legacy cw20 token, an NFT lock system and a beta game launcher that lets users support sales of newly launched games.

Codezen audited C2X together with Oak Security. Technologies in scope: Rust, Terra and CosmWasm. We reported 15 findings: 4 major, 5 minor and 6 informational.

- Audit type: CosmWasm smart contract audit
- Technologies: Rust, Terra, CosmWasm
- Ecosystem: Cosmos
- Delivered with: Oak Security
- Report date: 9 May 2022

# Divigent security audit

Divigent is a treasury layer for x402 agent wallets on Base; this audit covered its smart contracts. Divigent provides adaptive reserves, just-in-time recall, venue monitoring and yield on idle USDC.

Codezen audited Divigent together with Oak Security. We reported 20 findings: 4 major, 7 minor and 9 informational.

- Audit type: Smart contract audit
- Delivered with: Oak Security
- Report date: 15 May 2026

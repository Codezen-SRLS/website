# Polkadot micro-sr25519 security audit

micro-sr25519 is a TypeScript implementation of the sr25519 signature scheme used in the Polkadot ecosystem; the audit covered Schnorr signatures over Ristretto, hard and soft HD key derivation, and VRF outputs with proofs.

Codezen audited Polkadot micro-sr25519 together with Oak Security. Technologies in scope: Cryptography, TypeScript and Polkadot. We reported 11 findings: 7 minor and 4 informational.
